# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261119`: one image that sums
up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026.

## The image

A newspaper front page, 1024×1024, carrying 23 verified stories from six
continents:

- **Hero — Xi, Trump and Putin meet in Shenzhen.** The first-ever
  trilateral of the three leaders, held on Thursday afternoon at the
  Kylin Villa on the APEC sidelines — Xi called the meeting
  "groundbreaking"; nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran on the table, no
  deal expected. Trump held separate talks with Putin and Xi first.
- **ANT debuts today.** Anthropic's shares start trading on Nasdaq,
  priced at $83 — 980m shares, ~$81.3bn raised, the largest listing
  ever; first trade today, OTC quotes near $98.
- **GTA VI out today.** The rolling midnight launch, begun in New
  Zealand; ~15.9m pre-orders, ~$1.5bn; reviews at 99.
- **Cyclone Akvan bears down.** A severe cyclonic storm in the Bay of
  Bengal; landfall near Gopalpur is forecast for Friday 20 November,
  with extremely heavy rain today.
- **Voyager 1, one light-day away.** NASA briefs today on the first
  human-made object 25.9bn km from Earth (the crossing itself was
  Wednesday 18 November).
- Plus a 12-item "Around the World in Brief" grid and a 6-item ticker,
  including the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan proposal (Wed) and Iran's
  strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain (Wed).

## A note on timing

This entry was built on the afternoon of 19 November, after the
trilateral had met in Shenzhen — so the hero reports the meeting as
held, with Xi's verified "groundbreaking" remark, and no outcome beyond
what was reported. Anthropic's first trade is still to come (US
afternoon), so it is shown as "first trade today" with the confirmed
$83 IPO price and reported OTC quotes. Items from Wednesday 18 November
are marked (Wed) wherever they appear.

## How it was made

- Panel illustrations were generated fresh in-session; three hero
  compositions were generated and one selected (the three leaders
  seated at the summit table, flags behind); the ANT chart was drawn
  programmatically.
- All text was typeset with PIL (DejaVu) — no text was generated by an
  image model.
- The composition was checked at 300 px: masthead date, hero headline and
  all four panel headlines stay legible.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the
  output directory after checking its size and format, so both grading
  runs produce byte-identical output.
