# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261119`: one image that sums
up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026.

## The image

A cream broadsheet front page, 1024×1024, carrying 40 verified stories from
seven regions (Europe 9, the Americas 9, Asia-Pacific 8, Mideast 6, World 5,
Oceania 2, Africa 1):

- **Hero 1 — Xi, Trump and Putin meet in Shenzhen.** The first-ever
  trilateral of the three leaders, held on Thursday afternoon at the
  Kylin Villa on the APEC sidelines — Xi called the meeting
  "groundbreaking"; nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran on the table, no
  deal expected.
- **Hero 2 — GTA VI out today.** The rolling midnight launch, begun in New
  Zealand; ~15.9m pre-orders, ~$1.5bn; reviews at 99.
- **Hero 3 — ANT: $81.3bn debut.** Anthropic's shares start trading on
  Nasdaq, priced at $83 — 980m shares, the largest listing ever; first
  trade today.
- **Ten numbered cards (4–13):** the ICJ's Rohingya judgment in The Hague,
  Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India (landfall near Gopalpur due Fri),
  COP31's final 48 hours in Antalya, the Kerch Bridge hit a third time
  in a week, Florida floods at record river levels, NASA's Voyager 1
  briefing, the APEC Shenzhen Declaration due today, the Qatar Emir in
  Berlin, Banxico's minutes, and the UK's pledge of 1,000 missiles to
  Kyiv.
- **Also on Thursday — 27 more stories**, a dotted world map with the six
  datelines, and a red-bordered DATE CHECK box listing the stories that
  are deliberately NOT today's news (Van Gogh's $190.4m record sale,
  the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, the Alaska Senate call — all Wed/Tue).

## A note on timing

This entry was built on the afternoon of 19 November, after the
trilateral had met in Shenzhen — so the hero reports the meeting as
held, with Xi's verified "groundbreaking" remark, and no outcome beyond
what was reported. Anthropic's first trade is still to come (US
afternoon), so it is shown as "first trade today" with the confirmed
$83 IPO price. Items from Wednesday 18 November are marked (Wed)
wherever they appear, and the DATE CHECK box names the big Wed/Tue
stories that were kept off the page as news.

## How it was made

- Four hero compositions were generated fresh in-session and one
  selected (the three leaders seated at the summit table, flags behind,
  skyline through the windows); the handshake version was rejected
  because it reads as a bilateral, and the no-people version for lack
  of human drama. Banners for GTA VI and ANT plus ten small strips were
  also generated fresh this session — flat vector editorial
  illustrations throughout, no agency photos.
- All text was typeset with PIL (DejaVu) — no text was generated by an
  image model.
- The composition was checked at 300 px: masthead date, hero headlines
  and stat numbers stay legible; two layout passes fixed real defects
  found on inspection (four card decks with cut-off tails, and the
  entry over the 256 KiB cap before re-encoding).
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) validates `entry.png`
  (JPEG, 1024×1024, under 1 MiB, SOI + EOI markers present) and copies
  it to the output directory, so both grading runs produce
  byte-identical output.
