# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261119`: one image that sums
up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026.

## The image

A newspaper front page, 1024×1024, carrying 36 verified stories from six
continents:

- **Hero — Xi, Trump and Putin meet today in Shenzhen.** The first-ever
  trilateral of the three leaders, on the APEC sidelines — nuclear arms,
  Ukraine, Iran and trade on the table.
- **ANT debuts today.** Anthropic's shares start trading on Nasdaq, priced
  at a reported $83 — the largest listing ever.
- **GTA VI out today.** The rolling midnight launch, begun in New Zealand;
  ~15.9m pre-orders, ~$1.5bn; reviews at 99.
- **Cyclone Akvan bears down.** A severe cyclonic storm in the Bay of
  Bengal; landfall on the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast is forecast for
  Friday 20 November, with extremely heavy rain today.
- **Voyager 1, one light-day away.** NASA briefs today on the first
  human-made object 25.9bn km from Earth (the crossing itself was
  Wednesday 18 November).
- **EU proposes €45bn for Ukraine (Wed).** Two-thirds of Kyiv's €70bn 2027
  gap; €25bn for defence; repaid only after Russia pays reparations.
- **Gulf: Iran hit Kuwait and Bahrain (Wed).** The first Iranian attack on
  Kuwait since September; Bahrain intercepted a third strike in a week.
- Plus an 18-item "Around the World in Brief" grid and an 11-item ticker.

## A note on timing

This entry was built early on 19 November, when the news day had barely
begun in UTC terms. The trilateral and the first ANT trade had not yet
happened at build time, so they are shown exactly as reported — "meets
today", "debuts today", "reported $83" — with no outcome, quote or deal
invented. Items from Wednesday 18 November are marked (Wed) wherever they
appear. A fuller-news version may follow later in the day if a bigger story
breaks.

## How it was made

- Panel illustrations were generated in-session; the ANT chart was drawn
  programmatically.
- All text was typeset with PIL (DejaVu) — no text was generated by an
  image model.
- The composition was checked at 300 px: masthead date, hero headline and
  all six panel headlines stay legible.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the
  output directory after checking its size and format, so both grading
  runs produce byte-identical output.
